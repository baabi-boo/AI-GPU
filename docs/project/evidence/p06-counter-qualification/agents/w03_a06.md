## Three-stage execution recipe review

The modular route is feasible without invoking the historical five-stage local
executor. The new entry selects lifetime, dependency admission, and counter
qualification explicitly. All three scripts must come from the same clean,
reviewed new checkout and use its exact committed revision.

1. **Preparation:** `p06_dependency_local_prepare.py` takes `--source-dir`,
   `--workspace-dir`, `--expected-revision`, `--archive-root`,
   `--dependency-repositories`, and the chosen `--host-bin`. The owned workspace
   must already exist. Source, workspace, archives and repository inputs must
   be canonical and pairwise non-overlapping. All locked submodules and Git
   objects must be present. `workspace/tools` and the preparation receipt must
   be fresh. The stage writes `workspace/evidence/dependency-preparation.json`.
2. **Dependency build:** `p06_dependency_build.py` takes the same source,
   revision, workspace and host-bin, plus `--preparation` and an unused
   `--output-dir workspace/evidence/dependency-build`. It validates and retains
   preparation as `dependency-build/preparation.json`. Its source/build staging
   directories must be fresh. The report hash must be computed from this new
   stage's completed `report.json`.
3. **Qualified run:** invoke `p06_counter_qualification_execute.py run` with
   `--tools-dir workspace/tools`, fresh `--work-dir workspace/work`, fresh
   `--output-dir workspace/evidence/native-qualification`, the source/revision,
   chosen host-bin, dependency receipt and its fresh digest, frozen qualification
   proof and digest, and both original prerequisite archives and their pins.
   Preserve the bounded `--jobs 2`, `--timeout-seconds 1800`, and
   `--run-timeout-seconds 180` settings. The executor creates its own work/home
   and work/tmp; any separately reused outer local environment needs its
   local-home/local-tmp directories created by that supervisor.
