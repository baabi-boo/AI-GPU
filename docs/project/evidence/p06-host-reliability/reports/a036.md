## Change and compatibility

`_VerifiedReader` now retains its raw source descriptor throughout its lifetime
and creates its unbuffered stream with `closefd=False`. A rejected or partially
constructed stream therefore cannot ambiguously acquire and consume ownership
of the source descriptor. Constructor cleanup preserves the original
`BaseException`, including cancellation.

Close detaches the stream, source descriptor and parent descriptor before
attempting each cleanup once. It continues through cleanup exceptions, preserves
the first error on a successful scope, and never retries a descriptor number
that a failed close may already have released and reused. Repeated close is
idempotent. Two reader scopes explicitly suppress secondary cleanup failures
only after their read/verification operation failed. A038 independently
reproduced the original masking in eight failure combinations before root
approved these companion changes.
