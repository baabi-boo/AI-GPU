## Findings and disposition

**A034 F2 resolved — high confidence, static proof plus behavioral controls.**
The reader retains its raw descriptor in `self.fd`; `closefd=False` makes the
wrapper non-owning even if construction fails after creating it. Constructor
cleanup catches `BaseException`, attempts every owned release, and preserves
the original exception. Close detaches stream, source and parent before any
release, attempts each once, and preserves the first cleanup failure when
there is no earlier body failure. A later close cannot act on their reused
descriptor numbers. Source and parent remain held through digest and named-path
verification.

**Adjacent F2 confirmed and resolved — high confidence, static proof plus
reproducer.** Originally, an inventory read/verify exception was replaced by a
consuming parent-close exception. My independent synthetic control reproduced
all eight combinations of read/verify, primary OSError/KeyboardInterrupt, and
cleanup OSError/KeyboardInterrupt against the retained old source. Both source
and parent were released. The revised completed flags preserve all eight
original exceptions; the tar scope applies the same rule. Successful scopes
still propagate close failures. This disposition concerns reader cleanup,
not a guarantee against every later failure elsewhere in encryption.

**No unresolved F2/F3 finding in the reviewed repair.** The new eight-method
test module uses child-isolated audit hooks and descriptor limits, real raw
descriptors, consuming-close faults, exact-number reuse, repeat close, inventory
and tar scopes, and normal-close error propagation. Its controls test observable
ownership rather than duplicating implementation logic.
