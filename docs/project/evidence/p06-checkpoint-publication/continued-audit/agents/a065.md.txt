## What an eventual successful automatic run establishes

The `P06 Warm Workspace` workflow is master-push/PR/path triggered and also has
manual dispatch, selecting Ubuntu 22.04, pinned CPython 3.12.14, SimX and both
formats. It verifies the provider SHA through P04 preparation, then invokes
`p06_warm_execute.py run`; its artifact upload runs with `if: always()`
(`.github/workflows/p06-warm-workspace.yml:3–71,81–96,125–145,174–207`).

The ordinary v1 experiment runs **24 fresh processes, 792 launches and 25,344
checked FP32 words**: FP16 then BF16, six ordered pairs per format, with one
excluded warm-up pair and five measured pairs. Each process has one prime and
32 serial operations. The primary measured population is **20 processes, 640
operations and 20,480 words**. Reference and reusable-buffer policies share the
same executable/kernel per format. The raw verifier checks exact output words,
dispatch, operation/owner completion, allocation/release accounting, ordered
snapshots and the reported counter boundaries. The 320-byte tensor peak is
requested A/B/D payload, not physical-device capacity or host RSS
(`p06_warm_execute.py:3–11,42–75,279–295,368–440`; `p06_warm_records.py:85–209`;
`docs/designs/warm_workspace_benchmark_v1.md:47–105,107–146`).

Source snapshots bind the clean checkout, imported local helpers and selected
source/configuration bodies. Build and process qualification IDs bind contract,
fixtures, original compiler records, generated/effective headers, tools,
identified dependency inputs, artifacts and complete sampling plan. Source,
tools, dependencies and products are checked around execution; raw logs and
retained source/build/snapshot bytes are checked at finalization
(`p06_warm_execute.py:77–102,183–241,265–301,368–424,446–498`). Dependency inputs
are identified reused libraries; this runner does not establish their fresh

## Why this does not close the qualified warm experiment

The automatic route deliberately remains `p06-warm-workspace-v1`. Its runner
always leaves acceptance/performance/phase claims false and hypothesis
`not_evaluated`. Its separate analyzer **unconditionally returns blocked full
hypothesis qualification**, independent of favorable conditional host-time or
reported-counter statistics. The historical high-half finding cannot be removed
by relabeling a source revision or asserting a later repair
(`p06_warm_execute.py:143–149,524–536`; `p06_warm_analyze.py:38–47,71–84,237–259`).

The separate `P06 Qualified Warm` workflow is manual-only on trusted master. It
requires private control delivery, an explicit public age recipient, the exact
revision's full governance success, fresh dependency evidence, original and
extended counter proofs, diagnostic/control originals, and the explicit
qualified producer/reader. It reuses the same numeric population, but adds the
per-process counter/lifetime proof and admission obligations. Matching the
24/792/25,344 totals alone therefore cannot make an ordinary automatic warm run
a qualified warm execution or performance acceptance
(`.github/workflows/p06-qualified-warm.yml:3–20,35–64`;
`docs/designs/qualified_warm_workspace_v1.md:5–13,37–57,59–81`).

## Failure, publication and launcher boundaries

The producer checkpoints a planned command before `run_bounded`; a failed
prelaunch write stops before invoking that child. Command success requires an
actual started process, exit 0, complete cleanup and a bound unchanged log.
Processes enter the completed population only after raw-output and stability
verification. Counts sum the actually verified operation records; a failed or
partial population cannot become `full_matrix_completed`. Failure is retained
without sample replacement or retry, with final source/tool/dependency/log and
resource checks (`p06_warm_execute.py:116–121,171–213,388–498`). The unchanged
15 GiB disk and 4 GiB memory admission still applies (`:255–264`).

The shared-writer repair applies directly to this producer's report and snapshot
publication and to P04 preparation (`p06_warm_execute.py:25,161–168`;
`p04_ci_prepare.py:200`). It enables a later existing final write after cleanup
of an owned failed temporary; it does not retry native work. The initial write
at `p06_warm_execute.py:253` still precedes its protected execution block and
all native work. Persistent/repeated publication faults or that initial failure
can still prevent a final report; this audit makes no universal durable-closeout
claim.

Native calls go directly to `ai_warm_workspace` through `run_bounded`; this
workflow never invokes `vortex_ai.launch` (`p06_warm_execute.py:202–203,403–407`).
Its green status would not prove the new installed launcher's PID-directed
SIGTERM handling or native cancellation/cleanup.
