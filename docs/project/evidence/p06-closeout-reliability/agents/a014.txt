Conclusions
1. Failure retention: closeout_observation catches Exception and KeyboardInterrupt,
   revokes native_success and orchestration_passed before any retry, and retains
   only the failure stage and exception type. A later successful observation does
   not restore success. Packaging consequently selects partial mode and the final
   outcome remains fail after any caught closeout error.
2. Truthful execution status: native_reconciled snapshots the already reconciled
   native result after source closeout and before writer observation. A subsequent
   writer interruption cannot erase a previously validated run. An unreconciled,
   potentially started supervisor becomes unknown; re-observation is allowed only
   when sources are stable and writer completion is established. Confirmed run,
   not_run and unknown remain distinct. Missing launch/cleanup observations do
   not acquire a positive native claim.
3. Bounds and side effects: each observer is invoked at most twice. An ordinary
   exception stops immediately; only a first KeyboardInterrupt permits another
   read. The only call sites are pure writer/native receipt observations. Native
   producers, acquisition, packaging, encryption and final writes are not retried.
4. Cancellation nesting: the helper installs no signal handler. The existing
   hosted CLI decorator still owns cooperative SIGTERM outside run_bounded, and
   run_bounded retains its nested child-cleanup and restoration ownership. The
   new real-SIGTERM controls explicitly verify SIGTERM and SIGINT restoration.
5. Delivery gates: unavailable writer evidence defaults to False and blocks both
   packaging and encryption through their existing requirements. Unavailable
   native disposition defaults to unknown and permits only partial retention when
   writers are observed finished. No frozen population, resource gate, acceptance
   field or native producer invocation is changed.
