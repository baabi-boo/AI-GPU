## Discriminating coverage

- All four FP16/BF16 × allocation-policy transcripts preserve the exact original
  v1 prefix and original verified result. Their declared total remains 132
  launches and 4,224 result words.
- Explicit protocol selection: v1-only data fails the lifetime reader for the
  missing lifetime witness; the old reader rejects the additional protocol.
- Coherent successful legacy `gp` values `-1`, `7`, and both signed 32-bit
  endpoints remain valid.
- Exhaustive narrow-validator mutation checks cover all 66 run pending sides
  and all 594 MPM/flush pending sides. Representative first/middle/last mutations
  also pass through the complete transcript reader. Construction, all eight
  snapshot sides and destructor pending observations are checked through the
  complete reader.
- Every run reset boundary rejects skipped/extra generations. Missing, extra,
  duplicate and reordered runs fail even with repaired ordinals and unchanged
  favorable aggregate completion counts. Failed real-call outcomes and trace
  overflow remain rejected.
- Exact run/flush clock joins reject hidden ticks; the clock may reset between
  runs, and flushes may advance it. Counter decreases, ownership changes and
  unfinished disposal fail.
- Strict identity, route, false-claim, field/type, framing, JSON and record-size
  checks reject malformed inputs. A valid sidecar cannot override wrong output
  bits, wrong raw counter bits, an asynchronous error or invalid original owner
  accounting.

Two controls demonstrate stronger requirements without changing v1's scope:

1. Collapsing all 31 intermediate flush reset generations to the prime generation
   remains accepted by v1 but is rejected by the new reader at the exact flush
   reset check.
2. Adding a post-batch cleanup tick while coherently updating the terminal
   snapshot, its raw reads and destructor state remains accepted by v1 but is
   rejected by the new reader's final cleanup boundary.
