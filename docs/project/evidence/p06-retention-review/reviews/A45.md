### Inherited tensor DSP finding remains unresolved

`docs/project/p04-executor-audit.md` preserves **267 → 283 DSP**, exceeding the unchanged **5%** bound. The later independent `docs/project/evidence/p06-mpm-transport/reviews/broader-logic-gate-review.md` and `.json` corroborate the same failure in retained historical/current jobs: 13 of 14 default Beacon groups pass and tensor alone fails; the gate exits 1. The tensor row reports estimated 253.4 MHz and depth 25, not placed timing or a physical frequency measurement.

The review excludes the MPM decoder from that tensor hierarchy and identifies unchanged relevant tensor source/configuration/golden inputs. It does **not** establish the original introducing cause. A wider MX scale-index arithmetic change is a static hypothesis, not measured causal attribution. The required disposition remains matched historical/parent measurement and a correctness-preserving cause/fix or legitimate reviewed policy disposition. Do not attribute this inherited failure to new retention/automation or claim that it was closed by a later smaller green run. Do not update the golden or loosen 5% to hide it.

### Separate RV64 MPM decoder area finding remains visible

`circuit-review.md` retains paired RV32/RV64 × PERF-off/on Beacon and generic-Yosys data. RV32 is unchanged; RV64 restores missing high-half routes and changes Beacon LUTs **72 → 93 (+29.2%)** with PERF off and **1,022 → 1,439 (+40.8%)** with PERF on. Raw RV64 comparisons exit 3, aggregate `gate_outcome=fail`; raw builds report `NO-BASELINE`. Measured topological depth does not increase. The 24/72 generic FF-bit increase reflects formerly unobservable wrapper input bits becoming live, not new production pipeline registers.

The later `alternative-review.md` closes the **specific missing alternative-comparison F2**: a shared decoder saves 2/68 estimated LUTs but adds 9/21 generic cells and increases the PERF-off path 7 → 8. The direct decoder is retained as the tested depth-preserving choice. **Closing that comparison finding does not convert the original raw area failures into passes**, prove global minimality, settle full-GPU/extension/AFU resource use, establish post-route timing, or waive P06-A03. Keep this distinct from the inherited tensor DSP issue.

## 4. What the qualified-warm experiment can establish

Source: `docs/designs/qualified_warm_workspace_v1.md`, `docs/designs/qualified_warm_automation_v1.md`, `ci/project/p06_qualified_warm_contract.py`, `ci/project/p06_qualified_warm_analyze.py`.

A fresh complete attempt can produce source/build/load/counter/lifetime-qualified evidence for the **recorded SimX experiment** comparing `per_operation` with `reusable_single_tile` allocation under FP16 and BF16. It must retain **24 processes, 792 launches and 25,344 exact checked FP32 words**; its measured subset is **20 processes, 640 operations and 20,480 checked words**. Each process has one excluded prime plus 32 timed serial operations; pair zero is warm-up and five pairs are measured. Every new process independently supplies the required numerical, provenance and lifetime evidence. The earlier four-process qualification cannot be copied across.

Only a complete valid population is evaluated under the frozen predicates: each format's candidate median is at most 95% of the reference median, every measured candidate pair is no slower in host time, and qualified modeled cycles do not increase. No replacement samples, outlier removal, optional stopping, observer subtraction or threshold change is permitted. A valid retained original can yield `not_satisfied`.

The primary time includes submit, wait, exact comparison, explicit close, success checks and fixed observation bookkeeping. It is instrumented host time; modeled counter deltas are batch observations. Neither is physical GPU latency, useful hardware throughput, power, energy, single-kernel timing or an uninstrumented-runtime speedup.

A successful experiment/replay may support the next P06 optimization decision. It cannot, by itself:

- Close P06-A02's matching native-RTL/correctness/parity/inherited-gate obligations.
- Close P06-A03's chosen-target circuit resource/timing budget or dispose of either retained area finding.
