This sequence follows the [native handoff](../../p06-managed-preflight-handoff.md), [qualification plan](../../p06-mpm-qualification-plan.md) and [development audit](development-audit.md).

Pending reads decrease and reset to zero; they are separate transient accounting. A new 40-field inspector is unnecessary when the actual premises are established.
