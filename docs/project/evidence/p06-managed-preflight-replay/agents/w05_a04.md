## Source findings carried into the plan

`ProcessorImpl::run()` calls `launch_reset()`. On the admitted direct-RAM route,
`launch_reset()` calls `reset()`, which clears `perf_mem_pending_reads_`. The
constructor also resets once. The new validator should therefore bind the
observed construction baseline, require a reset delta of exactly one for each
of the 33 runs and a total delta of 33, and reject any intervening extra reset.
`SimPlatform::reset()` resets its clock to zero, so global clock monotonicity
would be the wrong invariant; clock comparisons belong within a reset generation.

The actual `ModelState` lacks a pending-read field. `model_locked` reads live
processor/core/platform state, and the wrapped `run_checked` records aggregate
entry/completion/error counts without a complete per-run trace. The existing
`_snapshots`/`_cache_flushes` validator accepts bounded nondecreasing reset
generations rather than supplying the absent intermediate observations. I also
read the first original process's retained witness as JSON: its four snapshot
generations are 1, 2, 34 and 34. This was a narrow data inspection, not a complete
independent replay or a new native execution.

The handoff preserves the source-bound theorem over 33 aggregates and 40 backing
components. Pending balance is a separate transient invariant; no new 40-field
inspector is required merely to restate monotone checked-storage premises. Actual
worker joins, final references, enabled-CP retirement, zero pending balance and
the no-later-update suffix remain necessary. It explicitly forbids reading
processor storage after destruction and distinguishes disposal clock reset from
terminal counter stability.

Fresh SoftFloat/Ramulator source-to-product and actual loaded-library provenance
remain a separate prerequisite. `third_party/Makefile` uses nested `make -j4`,
which must be reconciled with the declared native budget rather than assumed
bounded by an outer job count. The plan preserves the current 15 GiB free-disk,
4 GiB RSS, two-job/one-worker contract, the original four-process population and
the later unchanged 24-process warm population.
