## Concrete finding and correction

The preceding actual attempt `04` is a failed original at published
`146253db8407e21aaef70e05f3f0dd3c7f5f6d33`. After its separate map-size category
correction, the unchanged map has 25 selected yaml-cpp members and no selected
spdlog members, with both archive paths present as unique `LOAD` records.
[W10-A05's independent source/map observation](../host-diagnostic/review-04-spdlog-selection.json)
records the original primary-source basis: all 60 Ramulator object compilations
omit `SPDLOG_COMPILED_LIB`; 59 include spdlog's `common.h`, whose pinned
conditional selects header definitions. The seven separate spdlog compilations
define `SPDLOG_COMPILED_LIB`. Requiring an extracted member from that archive
therefore rejects the observed legitimate link.

The inspected production diff changes only these Ramulator requirements:

| Boundary | Required selected archive members | Required unique archive loads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Builder.verify_products()` | yaml-cpp | yaml-cpp and spdlog |
| `verify_document()` Ramulator link | yaml-cpp | yaml-cpp and spdlog |

The surrounding receipt checks still require both observed archive inputs,
their exact hashes and sizes, prerequisite chronology, complete ordered member
inventories, creator and index joins, and the exact source/workspace load
population. Every member actually selected from either archive must belong to
the admitted inventory and carries the corresponding compiled-object identity.
The correction does not make either archive optional.

The generic `validate_link_map()` required-member default, the native/model
SoftFloat selected-member requirement, numerical constants, resource budgets,
five source pins and qualification gates are unchanged. The revised design
describes this distinction explicitly. No numerical threshold, warning flag,
source recipe or test population was relaxed to obtain a passing build.

## Actual tiny build and meaningful controls

The [new regression module](../../../../../ci/project/test_p06_dependency_spdlog_selection.py)
contains **nine methods**. Its class fixture executes six actual host compiles,
three archives, two indexes and two shared links through the production recorder.
All thirteen actual records pass the individual inert record validator before
the scoped product checks use them. The tiny Ramulator-like shared object calls
one yaml-like object; its separately linked two-member spdlog-like archive has
no needed symbol and contributes no selected member.

The tests exercise the real producer product verifier and the actual inert
receipt's archive/link branch. They establish:

- The real map has a selected yaml member, no selected spdlog member and exactly
  one load of each archive. The real link records both stable input identities.
- The producer admits these observed product bytes while preserving the complete
  unused archive inventory.
- Removing, duplicating or replacing the spdlog `LOAD` fails both producer and
  inert-receipt admission. The inert mutations rehash their raw map, record and
  retained-inventory metadata before reaching the semantic rejection.
- Omitting yaml selection and naming an unadmitted archive, yaml member or spdlog
  member still fail.
- Reordering, omitting or substituting inputs to the unused spdlog archive fails
  the actual inert archive/creator checks.
- Substituting the unused archive's identity at the actual link fails the
  input-to-product join even with matching before/after declarations.
- The generic required-archive default still rejects a known archive that has
  no selected member.

### Explicit fixture limitations

The tiny fixture is not the 406-unit pinned graph. To reach the real
`verify_document()` archive/link callsite, its entry fixture substitutes five
upstream boundaries: `_verify_outer_commands`, `_validate_record`,
`_verify_compile_graph`, `_verify_generated_build` and `_verify_source_stability`.
Preparation/checkout summaries are synthetic, and 400 explicitly synthetic
compile rows satisfy the entry point's fixed cardinality while only the six
actual fixture compiles feed the tested product graph. Those substitutes are
declared in the test module; they are not production changes or full-original
proof. Individual validation of the thirteen actual tool records happens
separately before these substitutions.

The archive parser, actual member bytes/order, creator/index chronology,
product bytes, expected link inputs, map parser, exact project load inventory
and changed producer/receipt callsites remain real in this scoped check.
`subprocess.Popen` and producer-path opening are replaced with rejecting
sentinels during inert admission. A fixture success under substituted upstream
premises is not a claim that formal preparation, the complete graph, staging or
native execution occurred.
