Sources: `docs/project/status.md:44–62`; `docs/designs/managed_counter_lifetime_v1.md:35–52,91–174`.

Those results leave four distinct gaps:

1. **Boundary observations are not the complete component argument.** The live model record contains core cycles/instructions, pending balance, owner/generation, idle/running state, clock/reset generation and legacy `gp`. It does not directly trace all 40 cumulative backing fields through every updater, reset and finalizer. Its source-bound conclusion must therefore consume the reviewed component map, exact configuration, real compile/link inputs and loaded products. It cannot obtain that conclusion by counting successful launches. Sources: `tests/regression/ai_counter_preflight/hooks.cpp:57–82`; `docs/project/evidence/p06-native-lifetime/reviews/w05_a01.md:72–111`.

2. **Pending is a transient balance, not component 41.** Actual read requests increment `ProcessorImpl::perf_mem_pending_reads_`, responses decrement it and the direct-RAM reset assigns zero. Checking only after reset could conceal unfinished work. The witness correctly observes zero before every real reset-bearing call, but the complete argument must also bind matching request/response paths, the exact reset path, terminal drain and failure behavior to the actual binary. The accessor probe establishes sensitivity, not every other premise. Sources: `sim/simx/processor.cpp:201–212,382–400,429–449`; `docs/designs/managed_counter_lifetime_v1.md:97–131,158–190`.

3. **Checked fields alone do not bound every mathematical aggregate throughout its lifetime.** There are 26 single-component selectors and seven two-component selectors: six D-cache sums and one TCU-buffer sum. Two components may each fit while their sum does not. `PerfCounter` supports assignment and subtraction; the type alone does not prove monotonicity. The terminal theorem additionally needs zero origins, only nonnegative checked updates, reset preservation, complete successful checked aggregation and a suffix with no later update. Small observed values, positive endpoint deltas, timeouts and seeded transport success cannot replace those premises. Sources: `docs/designs/simx_counter_lifetime_v1.md:62–174`; `sim/common/perf_counter.h:45–111`.

4. **The current evidence contracts deliberately do not authorize qualification.** Lifetime-v1 retains all three unresolved obligations and all seven false claim fields. The new dependency admission binds its bounded source/build/product graph to that same lifetime contract; its reader still reports retained consistency and explicitly does not execute a native process or establish complete qualification. A newly passing dependency result supplies one separately reviewed premise, without changing historical records' meaning. Sources: `ci/project/p06_managed_preflight_contract.py:37–57`; `ci/project/p06_native_lifetime_contract.py:23–24,56–63`; `ci/project/p06_dependency_admission.py:36–42`; `ci/project/p06_dependency_local_replay.py:282–302`.
