## Decision

The seed-free **application and model** build is a small extension of existing production targets. The trustworthy **qualification** is larger: exact link provenance, actual loaded dependency mapping and terminal lifetime evidence are independent obligations. Deliver the former concretely and fail closed on any remaining latter obligation rather than enabling historical warm-v1 or attributing another diagnostic pass to the full managed caller.
