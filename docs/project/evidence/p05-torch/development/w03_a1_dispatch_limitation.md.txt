The rejection guards cover the enumerated dispatch paths and original tensor
preconditions. Higher-level APIs can evaluate an ordinary eager call before
entering a detectable transform. In the pinned version, `torch.func.linearize`
evaluates its callable on ordinary primal tensors first; FX symbolic tracing
can evaluate the input-free fixed model as a constant; a scripted TorchScript
graph can invoke a registered operator. These contexts are outside this
version's qualification and may submit and finish native work. Call the
operators only from the qualified eager contexts. A later transform exception
is not evidence that no work ran; retained native owners and execution records
remain authoritative. Arbitrary user dispatch overrides and graph-break
behavior also remain unqualified.
