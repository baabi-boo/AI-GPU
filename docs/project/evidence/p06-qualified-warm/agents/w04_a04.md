**No concrete blocker found.** Four adversarial scenarios reached their intended
semantic rejection before performance analysis. The final chronology scenario
needed one corrected invocation because the initial probe used a noncanonical
UTC spelling; both original and corrected results are retained. This is four
unique scenarios and five executions, not five unique tests or native runs.

packet constructor, the shared native command/build/process reconstruction, and
