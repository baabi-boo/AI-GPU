## Statistical predicates

`ci/project/p06_qualified_warm_analyze.py:20-27` loads the original validated
manifest and exact 24-process plan, and requires both common format builds.
Every process must match its planned identity and position and have a passing
outcome (`:30-39`). Consequently, an omitted, substituted, duplicated, reordered,
or wrong-phase sample cannot alter the measured population.

The analyzer delegates its decision arithmetic to the unchanged
`p06_warm_analyze._format_result` (`p06_warm_analyze.py:203-234`):

- The population is the five measured **batch means** for each policy and
  format. Each batch's total is the sum of its 32 timed primary intervals and
  its displayed mean is the exact ratio `total / 32`; operations are not pooled
  into a 160-observation median (`p06_qualified_warm_analyze.py:41-48,57-64`).
- The median is the third of five ordered batch totals. Because every batch
  has denominator 32, comparing these totals is exactly equivalent to comparing
  their means. The 95% rule is an integer cross-product:
  `candidate_median_total * 100 <= reference_median_total * 95`
  (`p06_warm_analyze.py:207-209,224-225`). Equality passes, and no rounded ratio
  controls the decision.
- Each measured pair, indices 1 through 5, independently requires candidate
  host total no greater than reference and candidate modeled batch cycles no
  greater than reference. The per-format result uses `all` for each constraint,
  then `all` for the three criteria (`p06_warm_analyze.py:211-234`). A favorable
  median cannot compensate for a slower pair or an increased cycle pair.
- Both FP16 and BF16 must satisfy the per-format result; the new analyzer uses
  `all` over the two frozen formats (`p06_qualified_warm_analyze.py:69-73`).
- Exact rational numerators/denominators are retained. Floating-point values
  and the descriptive population standard deviation are display statistics;
  none enters the predicates (`p06_warm_analyze.py:87-107`).

The frozen manifest explicitly fixes the threshold to 95, all-format and
every-pair requirements, one prime, 32 timed operations, five measured pairs,
no outlier removal, no sample replacement, and no optional stopping.
`p06_warm_contract.validate_contract` compares the whole manifest to its
expected contract, rather than accepting caller-selected thresholds
(`p06_warm_contract.py:293-311`).

## Exclusions, diagnostics, and real record shape

The original raw parser reconstructs 33 operation durations and returns
`operation_durations_ns` plus `timed_operation_durations_ns = durations[1:]`
(`p06_warm_records.py:203-211`). Its counter interval is explicitly from the
after-prime snapshot to the after-batch snapshot (`:158,180-181`), so prime
cycles are excluded as well as prime host time.

The new record verifier returns exactly `{"warm": warm, "witness": witness,
"lifetime": lifetime}` (`p06_qualified_warm_records.py:60-80`). It passes actual
sample, pair index, and phase to the original raw parser, checks the distinct
qualified-warm counter/lifetime identities, and verifies their snapshot and
lifetime relationships. The analyzer reads `process["observation"]["warm"]`,
which matches this actual returned structure.

All 24 batches remain in the analysis output. Only `phase == "measured"`
participates in the predicates. Each prime duration, all 33 operation durations,
all counter deltas, batch total, rational batch mean, raw-log digest, build and
execution identities, and supervisor duration remain available for diagnosis
(`p06_qualified_warm_analyze.py:58-68,75-77`). The four pair-zero processes and
24 primes are explicitly reported as excluded. Supervisor duration and observer
overhead are not subtracted from the primary metric.

## Qualification and acceptance boundaries

The public qualified reader verifies the external pins, frozen proof, dependency
packet, and trusted-reader stability before calling `replay.run()`
(`p06_qualified_warm_replay.py:235-265`). That run reconstructs source/build
bindings, every command and raw observation, retained observations, complete
counts, stability checks, and complete packet membership before returning
(`p06_dependency_native_replay.py:563-595,631-708`).

In particular, each report observation is compared with the freshly parsed
original raw output before analysis (`p06_dependency_native_replay.py:590-595`).
Thus, using the report's observation dictionaries in the subsequent analyzer
does not trust an unchecked producer summary. Analysis occurs only after the
complete run and another trusted-source check, followed by a further stability
check (`p06_qualified_warm_replay.py:265-270`). Archive root closure is checked
before this call (`:286-299`).

The arithmetic function is deliberately internal: its direct dictionary inputs
alone do not authenticate an archive. No standalone dictionary result should
be presented as qualification evidence. The output states that limitation and
sets `performance_acceptance`, `phase_acceptance`, and `acceptance_claimed`
to false even when `hypothesis_evaluation` is `satisfied`
(`p06_qualified_warm_analyze.py:80-86`).

The historical analyzer remains unconditionally blocked by
`P06-WARM-RV64-MPM-HIGH-HALF`: `hypothesis_evaluation` is the literal `blocked`
and `qualified` is false independently of favorable conditional statistics
(`p06_warm_analyze.py:71-84,245-252`). The new route reuses only its arithmetic
helpers; it does not relax that historical result.
