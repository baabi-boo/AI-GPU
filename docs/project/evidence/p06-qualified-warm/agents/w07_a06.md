## Decision mechanics and failure handling

`p06_qualified_warm_analyze.analyze_population` requires the complete ordered
24-process list, successful processes, two common format builds, distinct raw
transcripts and one prime plus exactly 32 positive timed intervals. It delegates
only the frozen statistical arithmetic to `p06_warm_analyze._format_result`.
That helper uses five batch totals per arm; division by the same 32-operation
denominator preserves the ordering and exact median comparisons.

For **each format**, the candidate median must satisfy
`100 × candidate_median_total ≤ 95 × reference_median_total`. Every measured
candidate pair must also have no greater host-time total and no greater
qualified modeled-cycle delta than its paired reference. Both formats must
satisfy all three predicates. A favorable median cannot offset a slower pair,
a cycle increase or failure in the other format. Rational display fields and
floating population standard deviation do not select these decisions.

The producer's finalization requires the complete ordered inventory and all
complete counts, then retains final source/tool/dependency/proof/artifact/log
and resource checks before qualifying the result. An exception or interruption
marks failure; there is no retry/replacement loop. The inherited native reader
requires the finished passing report, exact counts and full process population;
the explicit warm reader selects 24 processes and invokes analysis only after
complete reconstruction. A truncated population is invalid evidence, not a
valid negative experiment.

The frozen contract and runbook require retention of the whole original,
including unsuccessful attempts. Failed samples cannot be omitted, replaced,
renamed as outliers or retried within the attempt. A separately identified
later attempt retains the previous failure. This review did not dynamically
exercise interruption; earlier scoped probes and the coordinator's canonical
suite remain separate evidence.

## What a future result would mean

A valid `satisfied` result would satisfy this predetermined engineering rule
for this source, host, SimX timing configuration, fixture and instrumented warm
workload. It would not supply a significance level, p-value, confidence interval
or probability of repeatability. Minimum/maximum/median and population standard
deviation describe five observed batch means; they are not an inferential model.
Fresh processes do not establish independent errors, and adjacent serial
operations can share cache, scheduling and simulator context effects.

Alternating nearby pairs reduces reliance on a single policy order, but cannot
remove monotonic host drift, scheduler interference, frequency/thermal changes,
persistent OS/compiler/runtime cache effects, or order-dependent carryover.
The measured 3-versus-2 order split and fixed FP16-then-BF16 execution order
remain potential confounds. These limitations should be reported, not corrected
through post-hoc sample deletion, observer subtraction or a changed threshold.

The primary metric is deliberately warm: owner/context setup and the prime
precede it. It cannot establish a cold-start improvement or full-application
amortization. Instrumented host nanoseconds and modeled core cycles are distinct
quantities; neither establishes physical GPU latency, power, energy, board
throughput, arbitrary shapes/formats, framework performance or P06-A02/A03
acceptance. The implementation explicitly retains false performance/phase
acceptance flags.

A valid `not_satisfied` result would be a complete qualified experiment that
missed one or more fixed predicates. It must retain which format and predicate
failed and all samples. The present **resource-blocked, unstarted** state is
neither `satisfied` nor `not_satisfied`; code and synthetic checks cannot fill
that missing native observation.
