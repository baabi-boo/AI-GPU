# W02 A04 — Complete qualified warm replay reader

## Implemented scope

Added `ci/project/p06_qualified_warm_replay.py` as a separately selected reader
for the complete 24-process warm population. It uses the existing
`p06_dependency_native_replay._NativeReplay` reconstruction chain and the new
qualified warm proof, admission and raw-record validator. Historical reader
defaults remain four processes, 37 commands and the original pair-zero argv.
The new route selects the frozen 24-process plan and 57 commands explicitly;
it does not infer admission from producer claims or reported counts.

The shared native reader gained only explicit route hooks for complete counts,
command count, native argv, raw-record dispatch and additional process-result
identity fields. Its source, compiler, model-link, dependency, loaded-library,
raw-log, lifetime, stability and member-inventory reconstruction remains shared.

Both format build bindings and every process binding include the exact frozen
warm sampling descriptor. Actual pair and phase identities enter the native
argv and raw-record verifier. The new source proof is independently pinned at
`172721c671ddaa79541dde9c37c154b5ab5a99b8abb0caabf2e07036dd429efb`.
Diagnostic and control prerequisite reconstruction receives the unchanged
historical proof; the new admission separately joins its reviewed source
projection to those historical prerequisites.

The public archive reader requires the exact `dependency-build/` and
`qualified-warm/` roots, optionally below one explicit prefix, and independently
selected archive, source-revision, native-report, source-manifest,
dependency-receipt, qualification-proof and prerequisite pins. It preserves
`native_execution="not_run"`, `retained_code_executed=false`,
`origin_authenticated=false` and all inherited false acceptance claims.

## Report decoder and analysis boundary

The new report-only view adapter permits at most 64 MiB, 750,000 value nodes,
depth 64 and 64-character numeric tokens. It rejects duplicate object keys,
nonfinite numbers, invalid UTF-8 and malformed JSON. Other archive objects
continue through the frozen archive helper and its unchanged bounds. The
larger report limit addresses the measured historical four-process report's
92,649 nodes and the projected roughly 394,000-node warm population.

The independent analyzer runs only after complete raw/dependency/build/member
and input-stability reconstruction. Reader/helper/proof identities are checked
before reconstruction, before analysis and after analysis. An unsuccessful
performance hypothesis remains distinct from complete packet qualification.
The historical warm analyzer and its high-half block were not modified.
