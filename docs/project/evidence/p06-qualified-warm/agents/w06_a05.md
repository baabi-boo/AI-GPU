## Independent review

The new scheduling predicate requires the exact raw resource-review pin and its
explicit `execution_ready=false`, `native_execution="not_run"` and blocked
resource outcome. A separately pinned coordinator receipt must identify the
source path, revision, tree, absent native workspace, unchanged resource guards,
selected supervisor bytes and explicit exclusion of native launch until the
suite finishes. Boolean claims use exact boolean identity; schema version is an
integer rather than a boolean.

Both native workspace and supervision receipt must be absent at admission and
again after the pinned coordinator review. The script also checks absence before
every command, polls during each running command, and checks it in finalization.
An observed native start stops the child process group and prevents a passing
outcome. There is no native launch command or automatic follow-on action.

The canonical `python -B -m unittest discover -s ci/project -p 'test_*.py' -v`
selection is unchanged. There is no partition, filter, skip or replacement test
list. Preflight, project structure, baseline packets and final diff checks remain.
The suite retains its 60-minute bound, the supervisor its 90-minute bound, and
logs their 64 MiB polled limit. No memory/disk acceptance threshold is added to
or removed from governance tests.

The earlier supervisor's exclusive output creation, atomic status updates,
source-before/after inventories, fixed revision/tree checks, interruption and
cleanup handling, failure/not-run separation and final failure aggregation remain
intact. Exceptions or observed concurrent native work cannot produce a passing

## Practical limits and remaining obligations

- No governance suite, native command, cleanup signal or process-monitor loop
  was executed in this review.
- Polling detects ordinary coordinator mistakes; it is not containment against
  a malicious process that creates and removes paths between observations or
  deliberately leaves its process group.
- The pinned reviews record a scheduling decision. They do not authenticate an
  arbitrary origin or establish that resources later became adequate.
- Source-root inventories explicitly do not certify recursive native provenance.
  Recheck clean source, resources and all native admission prerequisites before
  any later native launch.
- A future exit-zero suite result still requires reconciliation of its actual
  log against the independently discovered **4,527 IDs**, including runtime skips
  and failures. Collection alone and this review do not establish test success.
