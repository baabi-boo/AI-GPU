## Implemented scope

Implemented five new files only, on the working tree based on
`f80b58b06e7bcf734f3190f6db478fd11f0bab01`:

- `p06_qualified_warm_contract.py` defines explicit route
  `p06-qualified-warm-workspace-v1`, the unchanged exact 24-process plan and
  the separately frozen source extension.
- `p06_qualified_warm_proof.json` pins the original source proof and exactly
  one reviewed-delta candidate: the native managed caller. All 68 other
  original measurement sources remain byte-identical. It binds all ten
  conditional premises, the fixed warm sampling and complete count map.
- `p06_qualified_warm_admission.py` checks the fresh dependency admission,
  current 69-source projection, unchanged original diagnostic/control archives,
  exact caller-selected pins and before/after stability. Historical prerequisite
  projections remain explicitly nested under `historical_prerequisites`;
  they are never presented as the new measurement-source projection.
- Two new test modules cover the frozen contract, plan, fail-closed admission
  and late input changes.

The old proof, old admission and prerequisite implementations were not modified
by this assignment. Admission remains nonexecuting and does not grant warm,
performance or phase acceptance. Its ten source premises still require complete
fresh per-process evidence and independent review of the caller delta.

## Shared interfaces

The new contract exposes `process_plan(source=ROOT)`, `sampling_binding`,
`COMPLETE_COUNTS`, `contract`, `contract_hash`, `load_proof`, `validate_proof`,
`verify_source_projection`, `base_proof`, source-file inventory and proof pins.
The plan adds `sample_id=id` to the unchanged warm plan. The complete counts
are the historical per-process count map multiplied by six: 24 processes,
792 launches and 25,344 exact checked words, plus the complete expected
snapshot/query/half-reply/flush/unavailable inventories.

The admission mirrors the existing constructor and exposes `identity`,
`helper_sources`, `prerequisite_inputs`, `summary` and `verify_stable`.
`admission_summary` and `stability_summary` let the independent reader rebuild
those results after its own complete prerequisite replay. The three exact
prerequisite input descriptors are the original proof, new extension proof and
original public source inventory. Trusted helper inventory additionally includes
all frozen warm-reference inputs, checked on both source and trusted-reader
sides. Imports do not include the new reader, so there is no admission/reader
cycle.
