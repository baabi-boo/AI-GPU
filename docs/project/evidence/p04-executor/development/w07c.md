## Deliverables and method

Added the standard-library-only [dispatch auditor](../reviews/audit_dispatch.py)
and its [actual SimX FP16 result](../reviews/simx-fp16-dispatch.json). The auditor
uses repeatable `--packet` directory/report paths. By default it requires all four
unique backend/format variants, each with the five declared cases and two poison
repetitions. `--allow-partial` / `--selected-scope` explicitly permits fewer
variants and records the actual matrix scope. `--output` requires a fresh path.

The checks were derived from the public C++ records, shared argument ABI, graph
contract and runner packet writer. They independently:

- Hash every command log and referenced source/tool/binding snapshot; compare raw
  `P04_RESULT` objects with the report and reject duplicate/ambiguous JSON/framing.
- Recompute each canonical qualification digest, reconcile bound source,
  dependencies, artifacts and compiler-record inventories, and require stable
  clean source and stable tool observations within each packet.
- Reconstruct the 56-byte little-endian launch block from version, dtype, shape
  and actual addresses; check alignment, representable extents and disjointness.
- Verify three ordered native operations, nine writes, three launches and three
  reads per model; check observed completion, operation/owner cleanup, actual
  bridge slices and row assembly, and memory-counter restoration.
- Check all five event results and profiling queries per operation, dependency
  chronology and the producer's enqueue interval. Successful asynchronous
  submission may be incomplete; completion and cleanup may not.
- Recompute byte/launch/event totals from raw observations and preserve separate
  host and event-duration summaries. Frozen case deadlines and recorded process
  budgets are checked; nested/overlapping durations are never combined into an
  exclusive end-to-end performance total.

Mathematical recomputation and compiler-role/source validation are separate W07B
and W07D obligations. Recorded hashes are not authentication of an arbitrary
packet writer. This audit does not itself execute a simulator or accept a phase.

## W08C follow-up: timing consistency correction

W08C found an F2 in the independent auditor: it accepted a zero `advance_ns`
with positive nested waits, disjoint waits whose sum exceeded `advance_ns`, and
reversed A/B enqueue timestamps. All three also passed when report observations
and raw log records were changed together and the log size/digest were updated.
W07C reproduced these false accepts before changing the auditor. They concern
the auditor's enforcement; none of the 40 retained actual observations exhibited
these inconsistencies.

The exact original auditor was preserved first as
[a deterministic gzip](../reviews/history/audit_dispatch-86e7bd87.py.gz).
W08C's [unchanged original reproducer](../reviews/history/w08c_dispatch_probe.py)
and its [original false-accept output](../reviews/history/w08c-dispatch-probes.json)
are retained beside it. The historical reproducer contains the original
workspace paths and must be paired with the original auditor to reproduce that
historical output; it is not the current regression command.

The corrected auditor requires nondecreasing enqueue timestamps for the ordered
A write, B write, destination-poison write, launch and read in every operation.
It also sums the 16 disjoint host duration terms inside successful `advance`
calls: three submission elapsed intervals, three waits, three comparisons, three
operation closes, bridge conversion, bridge comparison, output assembly and
output comparison. The sum saturates at `UINT64_MAX` before comparison with
`advance_ns`, matching the producer's duration-counter semantics. Model creation,
model/owner close and overlapping event intervals are excluded. Equal clock
readings and zero-duration observations remain permitted.

The retained [current regression probe](../reviews/probe_dispatch_timing.py)
passed **40 targeted probes**: 37 record boundaries and three packet-level
mutations with matching rehashed raw logs. Coverage includes every adjacent
enqueue pair in all three operations, each of the 16 nested terms, equality and
one-nanosecond boundaries, saturated totals, zero/equal timestamps and excluded
create/owner-close durations. The [regression receipt](../reviews/dispatch-timing-f4c6a3-probes.json)
binds the exact auditor/probe hashes and the actual input report hashes. These
mutations are host-only auditor tests, not additional native execution evidence.

The corrected auditor also accepted all four retained `f4c6a3` variants, covering
20 cases, 40 model repetitions, 120 launches and 600 event/profile pairs. The
smallest actual `advance_ns` minus the saturated nested sum was **2,372 ns**.
