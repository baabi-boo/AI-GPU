binary replay environment. Native executables, tool binaries, three linked
prebuilt dependencies, and full recursive submodule contents are identified but
not copied. Their reported hashes cannot independently rehash absent bodies.

tools/dependencies, and the declared bounded runner environment. A later tool
install or a regenerated cache is not automatically the recorded input.

emulation, and physical FPGA evidence remain distinct. This receipt does not
claim performance, complete fresh-matrix qualification, phase closure, or a new
