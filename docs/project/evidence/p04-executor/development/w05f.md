## Reviewed boundaries

- Fresh work and output roots, containment checks and exclusive creation prevent
  reusing an earlier packet as a new run. The preparation helper also refuses an
  existing preparation receipt. Its early path/receipt guards can fail before a
  new preparation JSON is available; workflow job identity and raw step logs remain
  the diagnostics for that case, not a passing prerequisite result.
- Preparation binds the expected workflow revision, clean recursive source,
  pinned cache keys, and actual tool/dependency hashes. Cache names are input
  identifiers; numerical qualification still depends on the subsequent runner.
- The runner retains before/after source and tool observations, checks selected
  helper bytes against the loaded inputs, and repeatedly checks source, tool and
  dependency stability. The exact final recorded tool inventory is compared with
  its initial inventory, including the W04A correction. Dependencies are repeatedly
  checked against the retained initial identities; a separate final dependency
  snapshot body is not recorded by this runner.
- A command must report `pass`, exit zero and complete process-group cleanup before
  its output can be interpreted. Command metadata and raw log hashes are checkpointed
  before later model checks. Nonzero exit, resource failure, incomplete cleanup,
  parser failure or final provenance drift cannot become a passing packet merely
  because a log contains `PASSED!`.
- Model output must contain the exact case/iteration sequence, one final success
  marker, no failure marker, and complete strict records. The verifier reconstructs
  the ABI, exact comparisons, live bridge lineage and event chronology. The runner
  checks artifact identities immediately before and after each native invocation.
- Aggregate counts are calculated from verified observations after the selected
  variants finish. A failure after some cases can leave completed case rows but
  no aggregate-count fields, or a variant marked `running` inside a finished failed
  report. Those rows preserve diagnostic progress; they are not a passing subset
  that can be substituted into acceptance. Interrupted or stale checkpoints also
  require rejection unless the final report is finished and passing.
- Reports always retain `acceptance_claimed=false` and `phase_complete=false`.
  A clean selected variant may pass while `full_matrix_requested=false`; this is
  intentional and does not claim the four-variant qualification inventory.

## Required later aggregation of the four dedicated packets

The hosted workflow emits one selected variant per job. Its four successful
reports will therefore each have `full_matrix_requested=false`. Independent
qualification must verify their union explicitly:

1. Require exactly one compatible result for each of SimX/native processor RTL
   × FP16/BF16, each with five exact frozen case IDs and two poison iterations.
   Reject missing, duplicate or mixed-source substitutes rather than summing any
   four successful files.
2. Check workflow/run/attempt identity, preparation outcome and expected revision,
   clean source/tree/submodule identities, frozen fixture/profile hashes and
   preparation-versus-execution tool/dependency identities. Different variant
   bindings, binary hashes, addresses, paths and timestamps are expected and must
   be checked against their own packet rather than forced to be globally equal.
3. Rehash retained raw logs, snapshot bodies, source bodies and generated records;
   reconstruct qualification bindings; re-read the raw model records and reconcile
   them with the report. The declared summary counters alone are insufficient.
4. Recompute the required union: 20 case/backend pairs, 40 model repetitions and
   120 launches; 5,120 hidden, 2,560 bridge, 2,560 output-block and 2,560 assembled
   word comparisons; 76,800 explicit tensor H2D bytes and 30,720 D2H bytes. These
   are requirements, not results observed by this source review. Preserve failed
   attempts separately and do not count duplicate general-catalog reruns twice.
5. Keep host queue/service observations separate from physical device latency.
   Host waits overlap event intervals, and tensor-byte totals exclude module,
   argument/control and setup traffic. Separate independent acceptance remains
   necessary even when the complete union passes.

submodule identities remain necessary reproduction inputs; this is not a complete
